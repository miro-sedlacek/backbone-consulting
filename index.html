How it works

01

We figure out if there's a fit

A few short conversations, free of charge. You tell me what's not working. I ask questions. We work out together whether I can actually help — and if I can't, I'll say so and point you somewhere that can.

02

I map your specific situation

Not a framework applied from above. I look at how work actually moves through your business — what's manual that shouldn't be, what's missing, what's unclear — and design a solution for that specific situation.

03

We build it and hand it over

Implementation: the right tools for your situation, connected properly, documented, and handed over so the system runs without me in it. Ongoing support is available, but independence is the goal.
