Why this exists

Corporate consolidation is not an abstract trend. When competition disappears from a market, prices go up. This has already happened, repeatedly, across retail, healthcare, media, and professional services. The counterweight to this is not another large company — it is small businesses that run well, survive, and stay independent.

Most business advice is built around one outcome: scale fast, raise capital, grow until exit. This works occasionally. Those rare cases get used to sell frameworks, certifications, and coaching programmes to people whose businesses look nothing like those examples. The number of people who have followed the prescribed path and ended up worse than when they started is not a statistic anyone promotes.

Most businesses do not want to become multinationals. They do not need to. A business can be successful, sustainable, and genuinely useful to the people it serves without ever raising a round or appearing on a list. That is not a consolation prize. For most people, that is the actual goal.

Backbone exists for those businesses — and for people who are just starting and have a specific problem to solve, not a script to follow.

What this means in practice

I am not affiliated with any tool, certified in any specific methodology, or committed to any single approach. That is intentional. A certification tells you one way to solve a problem and treats other ways as invisible. An affiliation gives you a financial reason to recommend one product regardless of whether it fits. Neither of those serves the person sitting across from me.

I will not tell you what business to be in. I will not question the validity of what you are building. If you have decided to sell frozen carrots to homeless shelters, that is your decision — my job is to help the operation behind it work. What I bring is a clear view of how work actually moves through a business, what is breaking, what should not require human time, and what needs to be documented before it only lives in someone's head.

The worst outcome of reaching out is a short conversation and a slightly wider network.
