A note on flexibility

Pricing is a starting point, not a ceiling

The prices above are the standard structure. For the right engagement — particularly longer-term or equity-stage situations — I'm open to discussing alternative arrangements: equity, profit-share, or deferred payment structures.

If you're an early-stage founder with a constrained budget but a clear problem, reach out anyway. The worst outcome is a short conversation where I point you in a better direction.
